## How It Works

**From raw market noise to a trade you can trust**

Every idea passes through the same disciplined pipeline your agents run 24/7.

1. **Scan** — Agents watch price action, filings, news, options flow and social sentiment across your universe.
2. **Analyze** — Fundamental, technical and macro agents each build an independent view of the opportunity.
3. **Debate** — The desk reconciles views into a consensus score — bull case, bear case, and conviction level.
4. **Stress-test** — The Backtest & Risk agents simulate the trade against history and your portfolio's exposure.
5. **Size** — Risk Agent sets position size, stop-loss and entry plan inside your predefined limits.
6. **Approve** — You get a one-glance brief. Approve, tweak, or skip — nothing trades without a green light.
7. **Execute** — Execution Agent routes the order to your broker and works the fill to your rules.
8. **Monitor** — Sentinel agents track the thesis post-entry and alert you the moment it changes.

## FAQ

**Does Quorum trade automatically without me?**
No — by default every trade requires your approval. Quorum does the research, builds the consensus and sizes the risk, then presents a brief for you to approve, edit, or skip. You can optionally enable rules-based auto-execution for specific strategies, always inside your risk limits.

**Which brokers does it connect to?**
Quorum integrates with major brokers including Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, Charles Schwab, Robinhood and Tradier, plus data feeds like Polygon.io and TradingView. You can also run entirely in paper-trading mode with no broker connected.

**Is my money or broker login ever at risk?**
Quorum connects through read/trade API tokens with scoped permissions — we never see or store your broker password, and we can't withdraw funds. You can revoke access at any time, and the Risk Agent's circuit-breakers add a second layer of protection.

**Do I need to know how to code or build strategies?**
Not at all. The agents come with battle-tested default strategies. As you get comfortable you can tune risk rules, build custom strategies and backtest them — all without writing code (the Pro plan adds an API for those who want it).

**Is this financial advice?**
No. Quorum is a research and execution tool, not a registered investment advisor. Its agents provide analysis and surface ideas, but all decisions — and all responsibility for them — remain yours.
