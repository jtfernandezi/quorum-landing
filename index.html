How it works

From raw market noise to a trade you can trust

Every idea passes through the same disciplined pipeline your agents run 24/7.

01

Scan

Agents watch price action, filings, news, options flow and social sentiment across your universe.

02

Analyze

Fundamental, technical and macro agents each build an independent view of the opportunity.

03

Debate

The desk reconciles views into a consensus score — bull case, bear case, and conviction level.

04

Stress-test

The Backtest & Risk agents simulate the trade against history and your portfolio's exposure.

05

Size

Risk Agent sets position size, stop-loss and entry plan inside your predefined limits.

06

Approve

You get a one-glance brief. Approve, tweak, or skip — nothing trades without a green light.

07

Execute

Execution Agent routes the order to your broker and works the fill to your rules.

08

Monitor

Sentinel agents track the thesis post-entry and alert you the moment it changes.
